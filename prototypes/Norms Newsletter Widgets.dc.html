1iOS widgets — Norm’s Newsletter, edition No. 048

The widget family, built from the same paper, ink and oxide as the app. One rule holds the whole set together: a widget is a printed slip, not a dashboard. Masthead micro-rule at the top, one job per widget, hairlines instead of cards, no gradients and no icons inside the surface. The only radius in the system is the one iOS forces on the container — everything inside stays square. 1a is the home screen with a swipeable stack; 1b is the lock screen with the audio Live Activity; 1c is the family at true point size; 1d covers StandBy and the Dynamic Island; 1e is the app icon. Play buttons work, the stack swipes (drag it or tap the dots), and tapping the top widget on the home screen opens the app.

Rules for the set

One job per widget. Front page, glance, count, next drop, audio. A widget that does two of them does neither at 170pt.

The radius is the system’s, not ours. 24pt on the container; every rule, tile and divider inside stays square, as on the site.

Type floor is 8.5pt mono / 11.5pt sans. Below that the metadata line goes first — never the headline.

Clamp, don’t shrink. Headlines keep their size and cut at 3 lines (medium) or 5 (small). No auto-fit type.

Lock screen inverts. Paper fill is replaced by vibrant glass and oxide lifts to #8FBF9E; that pairing is lock-screen and StandBy only.

Refresh on the edition, not the clock. One timeline entry at the 6:00 am ET drop; the countdown small is the only widget that ticks.
