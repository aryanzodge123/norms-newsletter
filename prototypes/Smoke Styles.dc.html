Six ways the mug steams

Same mug mark, six steam treatments. Shown at chat scale on the left of each card and enlarged on the right.

01 · in use today Dash drift Dashes travel up fixed curves. Mechanical, legible at 46px.
02 Rising wisps Whole strokes lift and dissolve on staggered offsets. Airiest of the set.
03 Sway Continuous strokes rock from the rim on mismatched periods. Calm, never restarts.
04 Puffs Dots grow as they climb. Playful; reads as bubbles more than heat.
05 Drift + draft Today's dashes, plus a slow sideways draft per wisp. Same read, less rigid.
06 Tall curl One long curl carries the motion; two stubs breathe beside it. Most editorial.
